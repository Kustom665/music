Foresight Kit

Six small instruments for reasoning about what happens next: extend a trend, simulate a range, update on evidence, compare bets, project growth, and score your own forecasts. Everything runs in this page.

Trend forecast

Extends a series and shows how fast the uncertainty widens. Auto mode backtests each method on the last quarter of your data and uses the winner.

One number per line or comma separated. At least 4 values.

Monte Carlo

Give each unknown a low, likely and high value. The formula is run thousands of times with values drawn from those ranges.

Use your input names. min, max, sqrt, pow, log, exp, abs are available.

Bayesian updater

Start from a base rate, then fold in each piece of evidence. For each item give how often you would see it if the claim is true and if it is false.

Rows marked "not seen" use the chance of the evidence being absent.

Decision calculator

One line per outcome: chance in percent, then payoff. Chances are rescaled if they do not add to 100.

Score = expected payoff minus risk aversion times the spread of outcomes.

Growth projector

Compounding with optional contributions each period, plus a solver for the rate between two known values.

Solve for the rate

Prediction ledger

Write a claim, give it a probability, and mark it yes or no when you know. The Brier score and calibration chart show whether your 70% really means 70%.

These tools extend the numbers and beliefs you give them. They do not know the future, and a tight range only means the inputs were tight. Log your forecasts in the Ledger to find out how good yours are.
